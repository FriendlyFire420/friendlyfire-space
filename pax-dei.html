Where we call home

Our clan, Friendly Fire, resides in the valley of Volvestre (Province of Ancien on the Selene server). Our castle stands just south of the Volvestre shrine — a quiet hearth for those who prefer warmth over war.

Keepers of the Eternal Hearth

We are Friendly Fire — a small circle of wizards and wanderers who keep a living flame in the heart of Volvestre. We are not a great army. We are not a hardcore machine. We are simply a handful of souls who believe the world is warmer when the fire is shared.

We craft. We build. We help one another stay standing. And when the night grows long, we keep the hearth burning so no one has to sit alone in the dark.

What the flame offers

New sparks are always welcome — whether you're brand new to these lands or simply tired of cold halls and empty promises. There is room by the fire.

The way of the flame

  • Play at your own speed
  • Come and go as you please
  • Help when you can, rest when you need
  • Treat others with basic respect
  • Keep the fire warm for the next person

How to join the circle

Message Masonfire in-game, or apply through the clan interface (press O → Find Clan → Friendly Fire). You may also simply walk up to the castle south of the Volvestre shrine.
