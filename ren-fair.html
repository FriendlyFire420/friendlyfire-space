Minnesota Renaissance Festival

Find us at the Ren Fair

Friendly Fire now has a booth at the Minnesota Renaissance Festival — a home for our handmade glass art alongside work from a rotating circle of guest artists. Come wander the aisles, watch the torch work, and take a little piece of it home.

Festival info See the glass

Our booth

This year we helped run and manage the booth. Next year it's officially ours — Friendly Fire signs going up and all. It's a space built around the things we love: glass shaped in flame, handmade craft, and a community of makers.

Alongside our own glass art, the booth gives other artists a place to show and sell their work. If you make something with your hands, come talk to us.

What you'll find

Handmade glass pendants, figurines, and marbles, live torch work, and pieces from guest artists — all under a timber-framed cottage in the woods. Stop by, say hi, and see what caught the light.
