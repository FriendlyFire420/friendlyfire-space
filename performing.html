What is Parafuego?

Parafuego is true fire bending. The flame loops, rolls and sweeps until it looks like one burning shape in the dark. It's slow and graceful one moment and roaring the next.

It's also very rare. Only a few people on Earth can do it, and when it's done right it looks like true fire bending.

Credit where it's due: Parafuego was created by Mikey (Michael Kaldis). Follow him on Instagram: @mikeykaldis.
